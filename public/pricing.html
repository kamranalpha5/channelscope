How tokens work

  • Map build: Depth 1 = 50 tokens · Depth 2 = 100 tokens · Depth 4 = 200 tokens. Expanding a map you already paid for costs nothing extra.
  • Trending 24h run: 50 tokens per fresh run. Viewing cached trending data is free.
  • Free tokens: 100 every day, refresh at midnight UTC — use them or lose them, they never accumulate.
  • Paid tokens: granted when your subscription starts and refreshed on every monthly billing date. Unused paid tokens do not roll over.
  • Fair use: even with tokens left, builds pause if the shared daily YouTube quota (10,000 units) runs out — they resume the next day.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Cancel anytime from your plan — your plan and tokens stay active until the end of the paid period.
