Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

ChannelScope is a YouTube competitor-mapping tool. It helps creators see which channels YouTube recommends around their own, spot breakout videos, and decide what to make next. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what we never do with it.

ChannelScope uses YouTube API Services. By using ChannelScope, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash (scrypt) — never in plain text, and never visible to anyone, including the site owner.
  • Usage data: the maps you build, the searches you run (counts only), your token balance, and your plan. This is needed to enforce daily token allowances and the shared YouTube quota.
  • YouTube data: public channel and video data retrieved through the YouTube Data API v3 — titles, view counts, subscriber counts, thumbnails, and publication dates. We do not access your private YouTube or Google account data, your watch history, or your subscriptions.
  • Cookies: a single session cookie (cs_session) keeps you logged in for 7 days. It is HttpOnly, so JavaScript on the page cannot read it. We use no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To provide the service: building maps, running trending scans, and showing your results.
  • To manage your account, token balance, and plan.
  • To keep the service within its shared YouTube API quota, so one user cannot exhaust it for everyone.
  • To answer support requests you send us.

Payments

Paid plans are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered on Stripe's own checkout page and never touch our servers. We receive only your subscription status (active, cancelled) through Stripe's webhooks, which we use to grant or remove your monthly tokens. See Stripe's Privacy Policy for how Stripe handles your payment data.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not share your personal data with third parties, except Stripe (payments) and YouTube (public API data for the features you request).
  • We do not show advertising and we do not use tracking pixels or cross-site trackers.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or device identifiers.

Data retention and deletion

Your account, maps, and token balance are stored in the site's database for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and its data, email us at the address below — we will delete it within 30 days. Note: the site runs on a free hosting plan, so stored data can occasionally be reset by a service restart; this is a limitation of the hosting, not a deletion of your rights.

Children's privacy

ChannelScope is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do, and significant changes will be noted on the site.
